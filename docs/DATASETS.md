# Datasets for Team B

## In use: Team B labelled evaluation set
- **Location:** `data/evaluation/cases.jsonl` (generated by `scripts/build_eval_cases.py`)
- **Size:** 34 consultations in Team A's transcript format.
- **Contents:**
  - Team A's real sample transcript (Oct 31, 2026);
  - the sample agreed by email with Team A;
  - the 12 legacy gold/adversarial cases, migrated and relabelled;
  - authored adversarial cases covering every safety rule.
- **Labels:** expected `safety_status`, expected conflicts (type / status / medication), and every expected entity with its assertion status.
- **Limitation:** the cases were written by the rule authors. They measure rule coverage and regression safety, not generalisation.

## MTS-Dialog (planned held-out set)
- **Source:** https://github.com/abachaa/MTS-Dialog
- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- **Size:** 1.7k dialogue-note pairs
- **Purpose:** Conversation-to-note evaluation, grounding experiments and a held-out test of extraction.
- **Preprocessing:** `scripts/convert_mts_dialog.py` converts the CSV into Team A's JSON format. Timestamps are synthesised from word counts and there is no ASR confidence. **Status:** the converter is ready, but the dataset has not been downloaded or labelled yet.
- **Train/Test Split:** use the authors' split; keep the test split untouched while tuning rules.
- **Limitations:** Focuses primarily on primary care and internal medicine.

## Synthea (planned)
- **Source:** https://synthetichealth.github.io/synthea/
- **License:** Apache License 2.0
- **Purpose:** Synthetic patient data (allergies, medications, conditions, vitals) for generating controlled conflict scenarios at scale.
- **Preprocessing (planned):** map FHIR records into dialogue transcripts with known deterministic conflicts. **Status:** not started.
- **Limitations:** Data is synthetic and lacks the nuance of natural human conversational speech.

## MIMIC-IV-Note (Optional)
- **Source:** PhysioNet
- **License:** Restricted (Credentialing Required)
- **Purpose:** Real-world unstructured clinical notes for testing terminology coverage and complex entity extraction.
- **Limitations:** Highly abbreviated hospital-centric data; requires strict credentialing and privacy considerations.
